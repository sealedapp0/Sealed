Thanks for using Sealed. This page is for help with the app, your account, and a capsule that did not behave as you expected.

How to reach us

Email sealedapp.help@gmail.com and include:

  • The email address on your Sealed account (not your password).
  • Your username, if you have one.
  • What you were trying to do, and what happened instead.
  • The device and operating system (for example, iPhone, iOS 26).

We will do our best to reply. If your note is about safety or a child under 13, please say so in the subject line so we can treat it first.

Delete your account

You can delete your account yourself:

  1. Open Sealed and go to Profile.
  2. Open Privacy and blocking.
  3. Choose Delete account and confirm.

That signs you out and removes your profile and the capsules you sent. If you cannot get into the app, email sealedapp.help@gmail.com from the address on the account and ask us to delete it.

Safety

You can block a person or report them from Privacy and blocking, and from their profile. A report goes to the operator of Sealed, not to the person you reported.

If someone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services first.

Common questions

When does a capsule open? At the date and time the sender chose. The lock is kept on our servers, not only on your phone.

I turned on Face ID. That stays on your device. We do not receive your Face ID data. You can turn it off in Profile.

I am not getting notifications. Check Profile and your device notification settings for Sealed. Notifications are optional. On a new iOS build, you may need to allow notifications when the app asks.

Can I edit or delete a capsule? You can change a sealed capsule you sent (including moving the unlock time later, not earlier) until it has been opened. After it is opened, those controls no longer apply.

Who can I send a capsule to? A friend on Sealed, or yourself. Search for a username to send a friend request first.

App Store or TestFlight issues

If you are testing a TestFlight build and cannot sign in, or a screen looks wrong, write to sealedapp.help@gmail.com with the build number shown in TestFlight and a short description or screenshot.
