This policy describes what Sealed collects and how that information is used. It is based on how the app works today.

1. What Sealed is

Sealed lets you send a message, photo, video, or voice note to a friend (or to yourself) and choose the moment it opens. Until that time, the contents stay locked.

2. Information we collect

We collect only what we need to run the app.

Account and profile

  • Email address. Your password is stored as a scrambled hash by our authentication provider, Supabase Auth. We do not see your actual password.
  • Name, username, optional profile photo, and optional bio. Usernames must be unique (they are compared without regard to capital letters). You can change your username once every 14 days. After you change it, we hold the old username for 14 days so no one else can take it.
  • Date of birth, used to confirm you are at least 13. The full date is stored privately. Other people with a Sealed account can see the month and day of your birthday (for example, when they find you by name or username), unless they are blocked. They cannot see the year.

Capsules and media

  • The text, photos, videos, and voice notes you put in a capsule, plus the title, unlock time, and the people you chose. If you reply to a capsule, we store that link as well.
  • Reactions you leave on a capsule after it opens.
  • When a recipient opens a capsule, we store the time. If that person’s settings allow it, the sender can see that it was opened.
  • Media is stored in a private bucket. Recipients can see a capsule’s words and files only after it unlocks.
  • We do not automatically scan the words or files inside private capsules. We may look at content if it is reported, or if we need to fix a problem, keep the service secure, or meet a legal duty.

Friends and safety

  • Friend requests, accepted friendships, and people you block.
  • Reports you submit about another person or about content, including the reason and any details you add. A report is a note to the operator of Sealed. It is not sent to the person you reported.

Notifications and settings

  • If you allow notifications, we store a push token so we can tell you when a capsule opens, when someone opens a capsule you sent (if they allow that), or when a reminder is due. You can turn notifications off in Profile.
  • In-app notification records (for example, that a capsule opened or was opened).
  • App settings such as theme, appearance, whether to notify you when a capsule opens, whether senders can see that you opened one, and whether to remind you before an unlock. By default, unlock notifications are on, and senders can see that you opened a capsule. You can change both in Profile.

On your device

  • A sign-in session so you can stay signed in.
  • Your Face ID or device-lock preference, if you turn that on. It stays on your device.
  • Drafts of capsules you have started but not sent yet.
  • Local reminder notifications scheduled on your device.

Technical information used to run the service

When the app connects to our servers, or when we send a push notification or an app update, our service providers may receive technical information such as an IP address, device or app version, and the time of the request. We do not store IP addresses in Sealed’s own database, and we do not use this information for advertising or analytics.

If you email us for support, we receive whatever you include in your message.

Information we do not collect

  • Face ID, Touch ID, and other device biometrics are used only on your device to unlock the app, if you turn that setting on. We do not receive or store biometric data.
  • We do not read your device contacts. You find people in Sealed by name or username.
  • We do not request your location. A photo or video you choose to upload might still include information your device attached to the file. Capsule and profile photos are converted before upload, which usually removes that extra information from photos.
  • We do not sell your information, show ads, or use your information to target promotions.
  • We do not use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs in version 1.

Camera, photo library, and microphone access are used only when you choose to add a photo, video, or voice note.

3. How we use this information

  • To create and keep your account, keep you signed in, and let you change your profile.
  • To deliver capsules to the people you chose, keep them locked until the unlock time, and show them afterwards.
  • To operate friends, requests, blocks, reports, reactions, reminders, and notifications, including letting a sender know a capsule was opened when that setting is on.
  • To keep usernames unique and to hold an old username for 14 days after a change.
  • To apply our name rules to usernames and display names only. Private capsule content is not filtered this way.
  • To enforce the minimum age of 13 and the rules in our Terms of Service.
  • To reply if you contact us, to fix problems, and to keep the service secure.

4. Who can see your information

  • People with a Sealed account can search for you by name or username and see your name, username, profile photo, bio, and birthday month and day, unless they are blocked.
  • Friends you accept can also send you capsules, and they see a capsule you sent them only after it unlocks. If your settings allow it, a sender can see that you opened a capsule.
  • People who are not your friends cannot see your private capsules.
  • The operator of Sealed can access data in the project the way any service operator can, including to handle reports, fix issues, and meet legal duties.
  • Service providers that help us run Sealed, including:
    • Supabase, which hosts authentication, the database, and media storage.
    • Expo, which delivers app updates and push notifications.
    • Apple (Apple Push Notification service) on iPhone and iPad, which helps deliver push notifications.
    • Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging) on Android, which helps deliver push notifications.
    • GitHub, which hosts this website (these policy and support pages).

These providers process data on our behalf. We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it with others for advertising.

5. Retention

We keep your information while your account is open.

If you delete your account, we remove your profile, sign-in account, settings, friendships, blocks, notifications, push token, and the capsules you sent, including their text and the photos, videos, voice notes, and profile photo you uploaded. You are also removed as a recipient of capsules other people sent you; those senders keep their own copies, including any files they uploaded. Some things may remain for a limited time:

  • An old username may be held for up to 14 days so no one else can take it, then it is released.
  • A report about you may be kept, with your account detached from it, for safety or legal reasons. Reports you filed are removed when your account is deleted.
  • Copies may remain for a short time in backups.
  • If a media file cannot be removed immediately, we retry shortly afterward.

You can also delete a sealed capsule you sent, or edit it (including moving the unlock time later, not earlier), until it has been opened.

Data that lives only on your device, such as drafts and your Face ID preference, stays there until you clear it or remove the app.

6. Your choices

  • You can edit your profile and notification settings in the app.
  • You can turn Face ID or the device lock off in Profile. That choice stays on your device.
  • You can block someone from Privacy and blocking, and from a person’s profile.
  • You can report a person from their profile, or report a capsule from that capsule. A report goes to us, not to them.
  • You can delete your account in the app: go to Profile, then Privacy and blocking, then Delete account. This signs you out and removes your data as described above.

You can also email sealedapp.help@gmail.com to ask what we hold, to correct it, to get a copy, or to ask us to delete your account if you cannot use the in-app control.

7. Children

Sealed is not for children under 13. We ask for your date of birth at sign-up and refuse accounts that are younger than 13. This depends on the date you give us. If you believe we have collected information from a child under 13, contact sealedapp.help@gmail.com and we will delete that account.

8. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to know what personal information we hold, to correct it, to delete it, or to receive a copy of it. You can use the in-app controls above or email sealedapp.help@gmail.com. We will not treat you differently for asking.

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, we use this information to provide the service you asked for (your contract with us), to keep the service secure and deal with abuse (our legitimate interests), to send optional push notifications (your consent, which you can withdraw in Profile), and to meet legal duties. Your information may be processed in the United States or other countries where our providers have facilities. You may also have the right to object to or restrict certain processing, and to complain to your local data protection authority.

If you are in California, we do not sell your personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, as those terms are used in California law. You may request to know, correct, or delete your information using the methods above.

9. Security

Capsules are stored in our hosted project, and the lock is enforced there. We use signed links for media and limit who can read a capsule before it unlocks. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

10. Changes

If this policy changes in a material way, we will update the date above and, when we can, tell you in the app or by email.
