This policy describes what Sealed collects and how that information is used. It is based on how the app works today.

1. What Sealed is

Sealed lets you send a message, photo, video, or voice note to a friend (or to yourself) and choose the moment it opens. Until that time, the contents stay locked.

2. Information we collect

We collect only what we need to run the app.

Account and profile

  • Email address and password (handled by our authentication provider, Supabase Auth).
  • Name, username, optional profile photo, and optional bio.
  • Date of birth, used to confirm you are at least 13. The full date is stored privately. Friends can see the month and day of your birthday so they can seal a capsule for it. They cannot see the year.

Capsules and media

  • The text, photos, videos, and voice notes you put in a capsule, plus the unlock time and the people you chose.
  • Reactions you leave on a capsule after it opens.
  • Media is stored in a private bucket. Recipients can see a capsule’s words and files only after it unlocks.

Friends and safety

  • Friend requests, accepted friendships, and people you block.
  • Reports you submit about another person or about content. A report is a note to the operator of Sealed. It is not sent to the person you reported.

Notifications and settings

  • If you turn on notifications, we store a push token so we can tell you when a capsule opens. You can turn this off in Profile.
  • App settings such as theme, appearance, whether to notify you when a capsule opens, and whether senders can see that you opened one.

Information we do not collect

  • Face ID, Touch ID, and other device biometrics are used only on your device to unlock the app, if you turn that setting on. We do not receive or store biometric data.
  • We do not read your device contacts. You find people in Sealed by name or username.
  • We do not sell your information, show ads, or use your information to target promotions.
  • We do not use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs in version 1.

Camera, photo library, and microphone access are used only when you choose to add a photo, video, or voice note.

3. How we use this information

  • To create and keep your account, and to keep you signed in.
  • To deliver capsules to the people you chose, keep them locked until the unlock time, and show them afterwards.
  • To operate friends, requests, blocks, reports, reactions, and notifications.
  • To enforce the minimum age of 13 and the rules in our Terms of Service.
  • To fix problems and keep the service secure.

4. Who can see your information

  • Friends you accept can see your name, username, photo, bio, and birthday month and day. They see a capsule you sent them only after it unlocks.
  • People who are not your friends cannot see your private capsules.
  • The operator of Sealed can access data in the project the way any service operator can, including to handle reports, fix issues, and meet legal duties.
  • Service providers that help us run Sealed, including:

- Supabase, which hosts authentication, the database, and media storage.

- Expo, which delivers app updates and push notifications.

- Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging) on Android, which helps deliver push notifications.

These providers process data on our behalf. We do not sell personal information.

5. Retention

We keep your information while your account is open. If you delete your account, we remove your profile and the capsules you sent, as described in the app. Some records may remain for a short time in backups, or longer if we must keep them for security or legal reasons (for example, a report you filed).

6. Your choices

  • You can edit your profile and notification settings in the app.
  • You can turn Face ID or the device lock off in Profile. That choice stays on your device.
  • You can block someone or report them from Privacy and blocking, and from a person’s profile.
  • You can delete your account in the app: go to Profile, then Privacy and blocking, then Delete account. This signs you out and removes your profile and the capsules you sent.

You can also email sealedapp.help@gmail.com to ask what we hold, to correct it, or to ask us to delete your account if you cannot use the in-app control.

7. Children

Sealed is not for children under 13. We ask for your date of birth at sign-up and refuse accounts that are younger than 13. If you believe we have collected information from a child under 13, contact sealedapp.help@gmail.com and we will delete that account.

8. Security

Capsules are stored in our hosted project, and the lock is enforced there. We use signed links for media and limit who can read a capsule before it unlocks. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

9. International users

If you use Sealed from outside the country where our hosting providers operate, your information may be processed in the United States or other countries where those providers have facilities.

10. Changes

If this policy changes in a material way, we will update the date above and, when we can, tell you in the app or by email.
